Linear algebra here is the mathematics of systems of equations in many variables, none raised to a power greater than 1. The course covers systems of linear equations, vector spaces, linear dependence, bases, dimension, linear mappings, matrices, determinants, quadratic forms, orthogonal reduction to diagonal form, eigenvalues, and geometric applications.

**Prerequisites:** One of MATH 139, MATH 141, MATH 151, MATH 200, or MATH 201 with a minimum grade of C, or a VCU Mathematics Placement Test score that places you into MATH 151 or higher.

**Quizzes.** A short quiz most weeks. Closed-book, closed-notes, no calculators or other technology. No electronic devices except a standard watch. Written on the assumption that you study at least 6 hours per week outside class. The three lowest quiz grades are dropped.

**Tests.** Two 50-minute in-class tests, on the course calendar. Closed-book, closed-notes, no technology. The lowest test grade is dropped.

**Participation.** Show intellectual involvement: working exercises, preparedness, dedication, and curiosity. Asking questions in class is one way to do that, and it is not the only way. High grades make occasional absences fine; otherwise, excessive absences can lower the participation score.

**Final exam.** Comprehensive over chapters 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, and 7. Closed-book and closed-notes. Written on the assumption of at least 6 hours of study per week for the whole semester. Tuesday, December 15, 12:30–3:20 p.m.

All exams and quizzes are on campus, in class, and in person. If the instructor has questions about work on a quiz or test, he may request a meeting before returning the graded work.

## Policies

**Attendance.** You are responsible for everything covered in class. Attendance is not taken, and absence is noticed. Arrive on time and stay for the class. Say ahead of time if you must leave early. **Web page.** Course information is on [RichardHammack.github.io/Math310](https://richardhammack.github.io/Math310/), not Canvas: syllabus, calendar, test archive, and other materials. Solutions to graded work are posted after the due dates.
